Frequently asked questions

Car broker questions, answered plainly

How a no-fee new-car buying and leasing service works, who pays us, and what we do for customers in New York City (all five boroughs), Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk), Westchester and New Jersey.

What does Valet Auto Leasing do?

We find, negotiate and deliver your next new car so you never visit a dealership. You tell us the make, model, budget and timing. We source the car, negotiate the price, handle the paperwork and bring it to your driveway.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There is no retainer, membership or fee. The dealer pays us when the deal closes, the same way a buyer's real-estate agent is paid.

If I pay nothing, who are you working for?

You. Our business depends on repeat customers and referrals, and that only works if we get you a fair deal.

Are you a car broker or a car concierge?

Both. We are a car broker backed by All State Auto Leasing & Finance, which has more than 10 years of dealer relationships. The experience is concierge: one contact, no dealership visit, delivery to your door.

Is a car broker worth it?

Usually, if you value your time and want a fair deal without negotiating yourself. We save money by getting a fair money factor and residual, removing add-ons you didn't ask for, and negotiating across multiple dealers. If a dealer's price is already excellent, we tell you to take it.

Do you lease and sell new cars?

Yes. We arrange new-car leases and new-car purchases, everything from a Camry to a Bentley, from about $25,000 to $250,000. The service is the same at every price.

Do you sell used cars?

No. We only lease and sell new cars. We do take used cars in as trade-ins, and we get you a real wholesale value for them.

What areas do you serve?

We serve New York City (all five boroughs), Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk), Westchester and New Jersey. We source new cars nationally and arrange shipping when the right car is out of state.

Can you beat my dealer quote?

Often. Send it to our free Deal Check. A trained person reviews it within one business day and shows you the true out-the-door cost, money factor, residual and hidden fees. If we can beat it, we show you how. If we can't, we tell you to take it.

Is the Deal Check really free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation.

How long does it take?

Starting takes six questions and about sixty seconds. Most deals close and deliver within a week or two, depending on the car and availability.

What happens to my trade-in?

We get you a real wholesale value, not a showroom lowball. With positive equity, you can put it toward the new car or take it as a check (we keep a small handling fee). With negative equity, we show you how to handle it so it doesn't surprise you in your new payment.

My lease is ending. What are my options?

You usually have three: lease something new, buy out your current car (sometimes below market value), or return it and walk away. We review your contract and tell you which one saves you the most, at no cost.

Do I need to give my Social Security number to get started?

No. To start, we only ask for your name, email, phone and the car you want. Your Social Security number, license and employer details are collected only for the final lender application, through a private secure portal.

Can I earn money by referring friends?

Yes. After your delivery, you can join our referral program, Valet Partners. You earn $150 for every friend who closes a deal. If you recruit other referrers, you earn $50 on each deal they close for 36 months.
