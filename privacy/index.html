Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Valet Auto Leasing, a division of All State Auto Leasing & Finance ("Valet," "we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on valetautoleasing.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Request forms (our vehicle questionnaire and Deal Check): your name, email, phone number, ZIP code, the vehicle you want, budget, timeline, trade-in details and any dealer quote you upload.
  • Credit applications: if you choose to apply for financing, the information on the application, which can include your Social Security number, date of birth, address history, employment and income.
  • Technical data: your browser type, device, pages visited, and advertising information such as the ad or search that brought you to our site. We use cookies and similar tools from Google Ads and Microsoft Clarity for this.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, call or message you about it, and find, negotiate and deliver your vehicle.
  • To submit your credit application to the dealers, banks and finance companies needed to arrange your lease or purchase.
  • To review dealer quotes you send us.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To meet legal and regulatory requirements.

Who we share it with

We share your information only as needed to complete your transaction: with dealers, lenders and leasing companies working on your deal; with wholesalers if you ask us to sell your trade-in; and with service providers that run our website, database, email and phone systems on our behalf. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages (SMS)

If you check the text-message box on our form, we may text you about your inquiry, such as scheduling a call, vehicle options and delivery updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

How we protect it

Your information is stored with secure, access-controlled providers and is transmitted over encrypted connections. Credit applications can be viewed only by authorized staff. We never ask for your Social Security number or account numbers by text message or email.

How long we keep it

We keep your information as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, accounting and lender record-keeping requirements, then delete it.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
