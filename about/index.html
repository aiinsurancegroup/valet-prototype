About Valet Auto Leasing

Your personal car valet. No dealership, no fees.

Valet Auto Leasing is a no-fee car buying and leasing service for new cars in New York City, Long Island, Westchester and New Jersey. You tell us the car you want. We find it, negotiate it, handle the paperwork and deliver it to your driveway. You never set foot in a dealership, and you pay us nothing.

Who we are

Valet Auto Leasing is a division of All State Auto Leasing & Finance, a New York auto brokerage with more than 10 years of dealer relationships. Valet takes that experience and adds a white-glove service: one point of contact, plain answers, and the car brought to you.

How it works

  1. Six questions, sixty seconds. Tell us the make, model, budget and timing. No Social Security number, no driver's license, no employer details at this stage.
  2. A real person calls you. We come back with options and real numbers.
  3. We shop and negotiate. We work across the dealers in our network to get you a fair price, a fair money factor and no add-ons you didn't ask for.
  4. Paperwork, handled. When you're ready, you get a private, secure portal for the final application. Never an open web form.
  5. Delivered to your door. Most deals close and deliver within a week or two, depending on the car.

How we get paid

The dealer pays us when the deal closes, the same way a buyer's real-estate agent is paid. You pay no retainer, no membership and no fee. If a service asks you to pay up front to find you a car, ask who they're really working for.

What makes us different

  • No customer fees. Ever.
  • Door-to-door delivery is standard, not an upgrade.
  • The honest Deal Check. Send us a dealer quote. A trained person reviews it within one business day. If we can beat it, we show you how. If we can't, we tell you to take it.
  • Trade-in equity check. We get you a real wholesale number. If you have equity, you can take it as a check.
  • New cars, Camry to Bentley. Lease or buy, from about $25,000 to $250,000. Same service either way. We don't sell used cars; we take them in as trade-ins.

Where we serve

We serve New York City (all five boroughs), Long Island (Nassau and Suffolk), Westchester and New Jersey. We source new cars nationally, so if the right car is out of state, we arrange the shipping.
